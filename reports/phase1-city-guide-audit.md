# Phase I City Guide Audit

This report will rank U.S. city guides by editorial quality and repair priority.

Scoring dimensions:
- boilerplate density
- exposed Wikipedia/source residue
- factual or geographic classification risk
- thin Things to Do descriptions
- duplicated prose
- weak overview, travel tips, or FAQ coverage
- tour inventory / commercial relevance

Already-paragonized guides are treated as controls rather than rewrite targets. Santa Monica is flagged for confirmation against the current guide implementation.
