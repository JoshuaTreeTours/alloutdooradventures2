## Interpretation

The automated rebuild/surgical threshold is deliberately conservative and is a screening signal, not an instruction to discard strong existing prose. Manual review should preserve high-quality attraction cores and remove only genuine weak or machine-padded material. In particular, New York and Portland contain substantial destination-specific attraction copy before repetitive suffix material, so they are better treated as surgical cleanup candidates than wholesale rebuilds.
