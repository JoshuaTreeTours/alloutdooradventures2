# Phase III — Engine6-weighted City Guide Implementation

Wikipedia/source-reference links are intentionally neutral. Phase III prioritizes destinations with meaningful native Engine6 inventory, but only changes guides where genuine content weakness or machine-generated residue justifies intervention.

## Authorized Phase III destinations

| Guide | Engine6 | Total tours | Treatment | Implementation |

| Florida — Fort Lauderdale | 25 | 28 | full rebuild | Rebuilt around the New River, Atlantic beach, Las Olas, canals, coastal history, museums and realistic water/traffic planning. |

## Quality policy

All seven enhanced guides are required at runtime to have an 80+ word destination-specific overview, at least three tailored highlights, eight substantial Things to Do entries, three seasonal guidance bullets, four practical tips and three FAQs. Attraction descriptions must remain substantive and unique and may not contain the known machine-padding patterns identified during the audit.

Wikipedia and other source URLs may remain as references. The quality audit no longer treats the existence of a Wikipedia source URL as an error.

## High-inventory guides intentionally left alone

Moab, Key West, Washington DC, Sedona, Honolulu, Houston, Denver, Las Vegas, Seattle, Los Angeles, New Orleans, Santa Barbara and other high-inventory destinations that passed the genuine-content screen are not rewritten in Phase III. Commercial importance determines where to inspect first; actual content weakness determines whether to operate.
