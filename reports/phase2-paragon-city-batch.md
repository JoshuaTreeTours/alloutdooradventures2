# Phase II — Paragon City Guide Rebuilds

Phase II rebuilds nine high-priority U.S. destination guides whose effective runtime content still showed stale, thin, boilerplate, or Wikipedia-derived structure after the Phase I audit.

## Rebuilt destinations

- Wisconsin — Milwaukee
- Minnesota — Minneapolis
- Pennsylvania — Pittsburgh
- Maryland — Baltimore
- Georgia — Atlanta
- Michigan — Detroit
- Utah — Salt Lake City
- Montana — Bozeman
- Montana — Missoula

## Editorial standard

Each rebuilt guide now supplies:

- a destination-specific overview of at least 80 words;
- at least three destination-specific highlights;
- eight substantive Things to Do entries;
- at least four meaningful sentences per attraction description;
- destination-specific seasonal guidance;
- practical travel and routing tips;
- destination-specific FAQs;
- an official tourism link; and
- no legacy Wikipedia/About-city payload in the effective runtime guide.

The enhancement layer explicitly distinguishes city attractions from nearby national parks, state parks, national forests, resort communities, and regional excursions so the guide does not mislabel geography for SEO convenience.

## Preserved benchmark guides

Las Vegas, Seattle, Portland, New York, Washington DC, and New Orleans were reviewed as part of Phase II selection and were not rebuilt here because their Things to Do cores are already substantial. Their remaining generic wrappers or legacy residue are better handled later as surgical cleanup rather than full replacement.
