# Venice Engine6 product selection

Destination: Venice (Viator `d522`). Catalog: `https://www.viator.com/Venice/d522-ttd`.

Selected 12 guided products with live public USD From$ prices, combined ratings, and unique 674x446 heroes.

The live catalog supports far more than 20 tours, but Viator DataDome blocking plus geo-currency dumps (EUR/CAD/GBP/CHF/AUD/SEK) prevented verifying many otherwise-strong SKUs as USD product pages. Jeep/off-road and stargazing SKUs were not captured as active USD pages. Helicopter `408100P25` had no complete USD rating/hero dump. Did not force inactive similar-options pages, non-USD pages, near-duplicates, audio/self-guided SKUs, 4.4 ratings, or products with unusable heroes.

Premium/high-value share is 10/12: Prosecco wine, private gondola photoshoot, private photo walk, luxury private island boat, Venice-in-a-day sightseeing, after-hours St. Mark's, private Doge's Palace and basilica, private Murano/Burano/Torcello motorboat, Dolomites semi-private day, and Secret Food Tours.
